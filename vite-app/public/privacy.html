Privacy Policy

VagaboNDE Field Ops · last updated 5 September 2026

VagaboNDE Field Ops is an internal application used by the staff of VagaboNDE (Grande Prairie, Alberta) to record radiographic weld inspection work: jobs, field tickets, hazard assessments, reports, crew hours and radiation dose. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what the application stores, where, and how it uses a connected cloud drive.

What the application stores

  • Staff accounts: name, work email, role, and the screens an account may open.
  • Work records: jobs, field tickets and their charges, hazard assessments, inspection reports and the PDFs filed with them.
  • Crew records: hours worked, mileage and radiation dose per person per ticket. These are visible only to the person they belong to, the office, and crewmates on the same ticket.
  • Client and contractor contacts: organisation names and the names, phone numbers and email addresses of the representatives we invoice and send approvals to.
  • Team chat messages, pictures and voice notes, which expire after 30 days unless pinned.

Records live in a Supabase project (PostgreSQL and object storage) and the application is served by Cloudflare. Email leaves through Resend. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running the business.

Cloud drive backups

An administrator may connect one cloud drive account — Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox — so the application can write a scheduled backup of its own records to a folder in that drive, and read those backups back to restore them. That is the only use the connection is put to.

  • With Google Drive the application requests the drive.file permission only. That permission lets it see and manage files it created itself, and nothing else in the Drive.
  • The sign-in token the drive issues is stored encrypted at rest in the application's own database, readable only by the server-side backup functions, never by a browser.
  • Backups are written to a folder named VagaboNDE backups in the connected drive. Each backup contains the records above and the filed PDFs. Older backups are removed on the schedule the administrator sets.
  • Disconnecting from the application's Admin screen removes the stored token. Access can also be revoked from the drive provider's own account settings (for Google, myaccount.google.com/permissions), which stops backups at once.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to write and read the application's own backup files and is never transferred to anyone else.

Client representatives

When a field ticket is sent for approval, the client's representative receives an email with a private link to the ticket. Signing records the name and email address they enter, the time, and the network address the signature came from, so the approval can be relied on. The link is theirs to keep as a copy of what they signed.

Retention and deletion

Work records are kept as long as the business needs them for billing and safety compliance. An administrator can archive and remove a year's jobs from the application, and can remove a staff account; an account with work filed against it is locked rather than deleted so that the names on past records remain accurate.
